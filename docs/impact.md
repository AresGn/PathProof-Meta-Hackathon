# Impact

No figure below appears without a cited source, and the nature of each source is stated as it is.

## Problem
A student in Benin often picks a program on vague grounds (the BAC series, family opinion, rumor). They cannot clearly see which programs are open to their real profile, or where to find the official information.

One precise point, from the *Guide d'information 2026-2027* (page 8): ranking is not done on the overall average but on a **ranking average specific to each program**, computed on three subjects with the series' coefficients. Few students know this. This is what PathProof computes and shows, with the guide page as proof.

## Impact figure (the one used in the video)
**77,101 candidates** sat the written Baccalauréat exams in Benin, June 2026 session (exams from 15 June, 140 centres).
- Source: Bénin Web TV, 14 June 2026, "according to the data of the Office du baccalauréat relayed by the online outlet Banouto" (« selon les données de l'Office du baccalauréat relayées par le média en ligne Banouto »): https://beninwebtv.bj/bac-2026-au-benin-77-101-candidats-repartis-dans-140-centres-attendus-ce-lundi/
- Nature of the source: a press relay of the Office du Baccalauréat data, **not** a page of the Office or of the MESRS. No equivalent official page was found on 3 Nov. Another outlet (La Nouvelle Tribune) writes "70 101" in its URL and "77 101" in its title: we keep 77,101, the figure of the article we read.
- Official counterpoint, previous session: 79,588 candidates registered for the 2025 BAC, Government statement of 16 June 2025: https://www.gouv.bj/article/3120/baccalaureat-2025-benin-serenite-recommandee-79.588-candidats-inscrits/. The two figures are not of the same nature (registered in 2025, candidates expected in 2026); do not compare them in the video.
